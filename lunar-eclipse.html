The Lunar Eclipse

Discover what happens when Earth moves between the Sun and Moon, casting its shadow across the lunar surface.

SUN EARTH MOON

01. Full Moon

A lunar eclipse can occur at Full Moon, when the Moon is on the opposite side of Earth from the Sun.

02. Earth's Shadow

Earth blocks direct sunlight from reaching the Moon as the Moon passes through Earth's shadow.

03. Red Moon

During a total lunar eclipse, Earth's atmosphere bends and filters sunlight, often making the Moon appear reddish.

Did you know? Lunar eclipses are safe to observe with the naked eye. They do not happen at every Full Moon because the Moon's orbit is tilted relative to Earth's orbit around the Sun.
