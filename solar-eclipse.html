COSMIC PHENOMENON 01

The Solar Eclipse

Discover what happens when the Moon moves between the Sun and Earth. Watch the alignment and learn the science behind this amazing event.

SUN MOON EARTH

01. Normal Position

The Sun illuminates Earth and the Moon as they continue moving along their orbits.

02. Alignment

The Moon moves between the Sun and Earth. The three celestial bodies line up.

03. Eclipse

The Moon blocks some or all of the Sun from view for observers in the shadow's path.

Did you know? A solar eclipse can occur only at New Moon, when the Moon's orbit brings it into the right alignment with the Sun and Earth.
